# Scorecard
1. Checkable results for 30, 60 and 90 days.
2. 4–6 weighted criteria related only to the work.
3. For each criterion a question about past experience and a small practical task.
4. A scale from 1 to 4 with an example of each mark.
5. Each interviewer scores alone, then discuss.
Do not consider age, gender, appearance, origin, family status, health or religion.
The agent prepares materials and collects scores. A person decides.

Answer in the user's language.
