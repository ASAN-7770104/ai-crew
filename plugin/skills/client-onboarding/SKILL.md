# Client onboarding
1. Card: company, contact, what was bought, deadlines, what counts as success.
2. What you need from the client: materials and access with a date. Do not write passwords into files.
3. Plan: day 0 welcome and the list of needs, day 3 first draft, day 7 first result, day 14 review and next step.
4. Risks: 3 days of silence, a change of contact, a scope dispute. One action for each.
5. Draft letters to the client; a person sends them.
