# Company setup

Goal: in 15 minutes get the context without which agents start making things up.

## Order
1. Check whether `company/COMPANY.md` exists. If it does, show what is outdated and update only that.
2. Ask one block of questions at a time, no more than five questions in a message.
3. Write what the person does not know as `TO BE CONFIRMED`. Do not fill gaps with guesses.
4. Show the draft, wait for a yes, save the file.

## Questions
- **Business:** what you sell, to whom, in which city and country, since when.
- **Money:** average order, sales per month, main costs, cash runway.
- **Customers:** who buys most often, why they choose you, why they leave.
- **Team:** who owns what, who decides on money and publications.
- **Tools:** where clients, tasks, money and documents are kept.
- **Goals:** three results for the next 90 days, in numbers.
- **Bans:** what agents must never do.

## Format of company/COMPANY.md
Sections with the same names, with the update date and the name of the person who confirmed it at the top.
Do not record passwords, card numbers, ID numbers or other secrets.

Answer in the user's language.
