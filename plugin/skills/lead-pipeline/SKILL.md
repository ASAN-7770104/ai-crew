# Lead pipeline
## Score from 0 to 10
- matches the customer profile: 0–3
- there is a reason to write now: 0–3
- the decision maker is known: 0–2
- a budget is visible from public signs: 0–2
8–10 write today, 5–7 this week, below 5 do not spend time.
## First message
A reason from the customer's life, one question, no price list. Up to 60 words.
## Rules
- Every lead has a next step and a date. Without them the lead counts as abandoned.
- The agent prepares drafts. A person sends them.
- Do not write again to those who declined.

Answer in the user's language.
